================================================================
  TI-84 Plus CE  --  switching between the two screen sizes
================================================================

There are two ways to run the calculator.


  FULL CALCULATOR          the normal window:
                           screen on top, keypad underneath.

  BIG CALCULATOR SCREEN    the calculator screen only,
                           filling the whole display.
                           About 4x bigger (1266 x 950).
                           Type on the Mac keyboard in this mode --
                           the on-screen keypad is not shown.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO SWITCH
----------------------------------------------------------------

Double-click one of these, in the same folder as this file:

    Full Calculator.app
    Big Calculator Screen.app

The calculator closes and reopens in the mode you picked.
Whatever is on the calculator screen is kept.

THE FIRST TIME you use one of them, macOS will ask:

    "Full Calculator.app" wants to control "CEmu.app"

Click OK / Allow. It only asks once. The launcher needs this
in order to close the calculator properly -- closing it any
other way would throw away your unsaved work.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  THE QUICK WAY, WITH NO RESTART
----------------------------------------------------------------

While the calculator is running, hold  fn  and press  F11 .

    press once  -> the window fills the display
    press again -> BIG SCREEN (calculator screen only)
    press again -> back to the normal window

This is instant and does not restart anything. It is the same
feature the launchers use; the launchers just exist so you do
not have to remember the key.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  IF YOU GET STUCK IN BIG SCREEN MODE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Any one of these gets you out:

  * hold fn and press F11
  * right-click anywhere on the calculator screen -- the
    File / Calculator / Docks / Extras menus all appear there
  * double-click "Full Calculator.app"


----------------------------------------------------------------
  RESIZING THE WINDOW
----------------------------------------------------------------

Drag any edge or corner of the window and the WHOLE calculator
now grows or shrinks together -- screen and keypad stay lined up
and keep the real TI-84 Plus CE shape.

This screen is not tall enough to make it much bigger than it
already is. For a genuinely big screen use:

    Big Calculator Screen.app

If the window ever looks wrong, double-click:

    Full Calculator.app

which puts it back to the standard size.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  SCREEN COLORS
----------------------------------------------------------------

The calculator screen normally has a WHITE background, which is
a lot of glare when the screen is magnified. This copy starts up
with that turned around.

    HIGH CONTRAST     black background, white writing, and the
    (cool on black)   graph colors changed to cool ones -- green,
                      cyan, blue, purple and pink. This is how it
                      starts up.

    HIGH CONTRAST     black background, everything in white.
    (white on black)  Use this if the colors are more distracting
                      than useful.

    ORIGINAL          exactly what a real TI-84 Plus CE shows.
                      Use this to check what a worksheet or a
                      teacher's screen is supposed to look like.

TO CHANGE IT, the quick way:

    right-click anywhere on the calculator screen,
    then choose  Screen Colors .

This works even in Big Calculator Screen mode.

It can also be changed under  Docks > Settings , in the
"Display" box, next to "Screen colors:".

The bar along the TOP of the screen -- the one that says
NORMAL FLOAT AUTO REAL RADIAN MP, with the battery in the
corner -- is left DARK with white writing. On a real calculator
that bar is already dark, so it is the one part of the screen
that does not get turned around.

Note: the graph colors are NOT the same as a real calculator's.
Red becomes pink, orange becomes indigo, yellow becomes aqua.
Blue, green and purple stay close to themselves. If a teacher
says "look at the red line", that is the PINK one here.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  IF THE PANELS EVER GET PULLED APART
----------------------------------------------------------------

In the menu bar at the top:  Docks

    "Screen"               shows or hides the calculator screen
    "Enable UI edit mode"  lets the panels be dragged around

Leave "Enable UI edit mode" switched OFF. With it off the panels
are locked together and cannot be pulled apart by accident.
